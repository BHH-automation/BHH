The Heritage Trail

Several days, one family, one guide

A private trail across England, Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland. We plan the route, buy the entry passes and walk beside you from the first castle to the last garden.

What this is

A single guided day suits a family with one free afternoon. A trail is for a family with a week. The same guide stays with you throughout, so nothing has to be explained twice and the route can change on the second morning if the children have decided they prefer castles to gardens.

We plan the route and buy the entry passes in advance, which is what removes the queuing and the timed entry problems that spoil a self planned trip. You book your own hotel and your own driver, because families have their own preferences on both. Everything else is ours.
