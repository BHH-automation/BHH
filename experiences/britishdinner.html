British Dinner

A complete traditional Sunday roast in a real English dining room, your host family at the table, your family only.

What this is

The Sunday roast is the meal the English keep for the people they care about. A joint of meat, potatoes roasted in the oven alongside it, vegetables from the garden or the local market, gravy made in the same pan, and a pudding afterwards.

It is served in the family dining room, at the family table, with the host family eating with you. This is not a restaurant sitting with a British theme. It is an evening in someone’s home.

Good to know

  • Held in the home of a host family we have met and verified.
  • Halal catering is available and the setting is alcohol free on request.
  • Prayer time is respected. Tell us and the evening is arranged around it.
  • Tell us about any allergy or dietary requirement when you enquire.
