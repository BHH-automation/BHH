Narrowboat Holiday

A multi day narrowboat holiday across the English countryside, a full canal boat break.

What this is

A narrowboat holiday is a week, or part of one, living on the boat. You steer it, you work the locks, you moor where you like at the end of the day. Families who have done it once tend to do it again.

This is arranged through a provider we trust rather than by us. We make the introduction and tell them what your family needs.

Good to know

  • Booked and paid directly with the provider, not through British Heritage Hosts.
  • We make the introduction and pass on what your family needs.
  • The provider’s own terms, cancellation policy and insurance apply.
  • Tell us your dates and the size of your party when you ask for the introduction.
