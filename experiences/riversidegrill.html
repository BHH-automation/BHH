Riverside Grill

A private day at an English riverside cottage from 10 am to 8 pm. Skewered meat over real charcoal beside a clear flowing river, your family only, halal available.

What this is

The cottage and its river frontage are yours from ten in the morning until eight in the evening. Meat is skewered and cooked over real charcoal beside the water, not on a gas grill, and it is cooked through the day rather than served at one sitting.

The day suits a family who want the children outside and the afternoon unhurried. It is also available for a larger private party of your own guests, arranged in advance.

Good to know

  • Ten in the morning until eight in the evening, the cottage and river frontage yours alone.
  • Halal meat is available. The setting is alcohol free on request.
  • Also available for a larger private party of your own guests, arranged in advance.
  • An outdoor day beside water. Tell us the ages of your children so the day is arranged safely.
