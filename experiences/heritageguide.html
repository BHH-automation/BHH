Heritage Guide

A personal British Heritage Guide takes your family through the most storied landscapes and history of the United Kingdom, for a day or a multi day trail.

1. What this is 2. Good to know 3. How it is arranged 4. Heritage Guide in pictures 5. Plan your private visit (Enquire about this experience) ← All nine experiences

What this is

Your guide is yours for the day. Where you go, how long you stay, when you stop to eat and when you turn back are decided between you and the guide, not by a coach timetable.

A single day works well around one city or one great house. Families who want more take the multi day Heritage Trail, which links castles, cathedrals, royal palaces, country houses, historic towns and heritage spas across England, Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland.

Good to know

  • Female guides are available on request.
  • Available as a single day or as a multi day Heritage Trail.
  • Entry to properties, travel and meals are arranged as part of the plan and quoted with it.
  • Prayer time is respected and the day is arranged around it.
