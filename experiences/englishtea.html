English Tea

Fine china, three tiered stands, homemade scones and cakes, the quintessential English tradition in a private home.

1. What this is 2. Good to know 3. How it is arranged 4. English Tea in pictures 5. Plan your private visit (Enquire about this experience)

What this is

Afternoon tea in England is not a meal so much as an afternoon. It is served in the sitting room or the garden of a family home, on the china that family actually uses, with scones still warm and cakes made that morning.

Your host family is at the table with you. They pour, they explain what everything is and where the recipes came from, and they answer whatever your children want to ask. No other guests are present.

Good to know

  • Held in the home of a host family we have met and verified.
  • Halal catering is available and the setting is alcohol free on request.
  • Tell us about any allergy or dietary requirement when you enquire and it is arranged before you arrive.
  • Suitable for children. Say how many are coming and their ages.
