Cultural Immersion Programme

Learn or improve English during your stay. Flexible courses with accredited London institutes, grouped by age, from a few sessions to a full programme.

What this is

Tuition is delivered by accredited London institutes, with students grouped by age rather than put together. It can be a few sessions across a fortnight or a full programme across a longer stay.

The point of learning English in England is the hours outside the classroom. The programme is built around tours, museums and cultural activities so that what is taught in the morning is used in the afternoon.

Good to know

  • Delivered by accredited London institutes.
  • Students are grouped by age.
  • From a few sessions to a full programme, arranged around your dates.
  • Tell us the ages and the current level of English of each student when you enquire.
