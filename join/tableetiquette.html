About BHH

About British Heritage Hosts

British Heritage Hosts is building a carefully selected private network of British hosts, guides and organisers in London and the English countryside. Our purpose is to open real British homes to international families for private teas, dinners, guided days and canal journeys, every visit arranged personally. We are accepting enquiries now from families planning a visit, and from hosts, guides and organisers who wish to join us.

The BHH Standard

Why families choose us

01

Exclusively yours

Every BHH experience is arranged for your family alone. No other guests share your host, your guide, or your vessel. Never.

02

Personally vetted

BHH verifies every host, every guide, and every partner before they join the network. Our standard is non negotiable.

Genuinely authentic

Every host we accept must have deep personal roots in English home life. BHH does not perform Englishness. It opens the door to it.

    We may collect: full name, title, and contact details; country of residence, city, and nationality; dietary requirements and allergy information; Enhanced DBS certificate status (hosts and partners); Basic DBS check consent, public liability insurance details and self employment status (Heritage Scouts); payment and billing information (processed by Airwallex); feedback data, where given; and any additional information you voluntarily provide.

    We use your data to: respond to enquiries and prepare proposals; process bookings, payments, and cancellations; match guests with suitable hosts; verify host, partner and Heritage Scout credentials; send pre experience briefings and post experience feedback requests; improve our services; and comply with legal obligations.

    3. Legal Basis for Processing

    We process your data on the basis of: contractual necessity (to fulfil services); legitimate interest (to operate and improve our business and protect safety); consent (where explicitly agreed, such as marketing); and legal obligation (to comply with applicable laws).

    4. Data Sharing

    We do not sell your personal data. We may share data with: approved hosts and partners (to deliver your experience); Airwallex (for payment processing); and professional advisors or regulatory authorities where required by law.

    5. Data Retention

    Booking and transaction records: 7 years. Feedback data, where given: 36 months, then anonymised. Unsuccessful applications: 12 months, then deleted. Marketing preferences: until you withdraw consent.

    Under the UK GDPR, you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or port your personal data, and to withdraw consent at any time. Contact us at info@britishheritagehosts.com.

    7. Data Security

    We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data. Payment data is handled by our PCI compliant payment partner and is never stored on our systems.

    8. Cookies

    Our website uses essential cookies only. We do not use tracking, advertising, or third party analytics cookies.
